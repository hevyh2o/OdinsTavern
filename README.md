# Odin's Tavern

Odin keeps a suggestion box by the hearth. The ravens read every note, and he reads
the ones they bring back. Some of them he even acts on.

This is where you tell us what's broken, what's missing, or what you'd like Odin to
do next, for any of the Odin's mods by BeardedGiants.

## Report a bug or suggest an idea

1. Go to [Issues](https://github.com/hevyh2o/OdinsTavern/issues) and click **New issue**.
2. Pick a form: **Bug report** when something doesn't work, **Idea** for a new feature
   or a whole new mod.
3. Fill it in. Each form asks only for what helps.

Questions, tips and general chat go in
[Discussions](https://github.com/hevyh2o/OdinsTavern/discussions). Odin prefers his
complaints and his small talk in separate cups.

## What to include with a bug

- **Your log.** `BepInEx\LogOutput.log` from your profile folder. In r2modman or the
  Thunderstore Mod Manager: Settings, then Browse profile folder. Drag the file into
  the form. It's the single most useful thing you can send.
- **The mod and its version.** The mod manager shows it next to the mod's name.
- **Where you play.** Single-player, a dedicated server, or a world hosted by a friend.
- **Your other mods.** A list, or your mod manager's profile code (Settings, then
  Export profile as code).
- **How to make it happen again.** Even a rough "it breaks when I log in near a troll"
  helps.

has it.
